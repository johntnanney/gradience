## Central question

---

## Safe stable Route 2 language

Use directly (with existing project scope bounds):

1. Evidence gating and conservative narrowing are stable workflow invariants.
2. Aggregation is a first-class decision seam.
3. Worst-case, distributional, and QA-dominant families are operationally distinct.
4. Workflow-level portability is stronger than metric-level portability.
5. The broadened substrate is real but narrow.

---

## Guarded-but-usable language

Use with explicit caveats and bounded scope wording:

1. Checkpoint triage is stable as a broadened workflow inside the validated envelope.
2. Same-task/cross-task directional separation is useful but not full-class parity complete.
3. Same-family intermediate and optional states are useful review categories with threshold guardrails.
4. Aggregation taxonomy is usable at coarse grain; fine thresholds remain non-canonical.
5. Behavioral profile distinctions and collapse-vs-contamination are usable as bounded explanatory language in tested merge-facing settings.

---

## Research-only or local suppression language

Keep out of broad product/public claims:

1. Optional/near-miss portability outside LoRA.
2. Routing-confusability non-transfer as a broad behavioral rule.
3. Collapse-vs-contamination as a cross-context law.

## Communication zones

### Zone 1 -- Public writing

- Use `core_stable_non_edge` claims plainly.
- Mention `stable_but_local` and `moderate_but_product_relevant` only in explicitly bounded methodology sections.
- Suppress `thin_suppress_public` claims.

### Zone 2 -- Product and alpha workflow docs

- Use stable core claims as defaults.
- Use stable-local and moderate-product-relevant claims with guardrails and no hard thresholds.
- Keep thin claims out.

### Zone 3 -- Internal architecture docs

- Include seam-level aggregation logic, representation-local metric semantics, and bounded behavioral channel distinctions.
- Preserve scope notes (`CPU-only`, `small encoder`, `shared base`, `classification`).

### Zone 4 -- Sidecar/research framing

- Keep thin claims and cross-context extrapolations in sidecar-only framing.
- Track strengthening targets without promoting them to product/public claims.
