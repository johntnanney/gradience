# TIES Spectral Bounds (Planned)

## Scope

TIES operations are entrywise (trim + sign election + disjoint mean), so exact
closed-form spectral expressions are generally not available.

## Bounded analytical goals

1. Derive expected singular-value attenuation under isotropic trimming.
2. Derive worst-case distortion bounds under adversarial trimming.
3. Characterize sign-election behavior in high-overlap negative-agreement cases.

## Validation plan

- synthetic matrix sweeps across overlap/agreement regimes
- compare bound tightness and violation rate
- keep claims probabilistic and bounded
